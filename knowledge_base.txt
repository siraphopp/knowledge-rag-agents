[SECTION 1: INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL, CHECK-IN & BOARDING GATE POLICY]
Passengers departing on international flights from Suvarnabhumi International Airport (BKK) must arrive at the terminal at least 3 hours prior to scheduled departure. Physical check-in counters and bag-drop kiosks open 3 hours before departure and strictly close 60 minutes prior to scheduled departure time. Online and mobile check-in is available from 48 hours up to 90 minutes before departure. Boarding gates open 60 minutes prior to departure and close strictly 20 minutes before scheduled takeoff; late-arriving passengers at the gate will be offloaded along with their checked luggage for aviation security compliance. All international passengers must hold a machine-readable passport with at least 6 months validity beyond their planned return date, at least 2 blank visa pages, a confirmed return or onward ticket, and any required destination entry visas or Electronic Travel Authorizations (ETA). Eligible passport holders (Thai nationals and select foreign biometric passport holders) may use Automated Biometric E-Gates at both departure and arrival immigration, provided they are at least 120 cm tall and not traveling with infants. Passengers denied boarding due to incomplete or invalid travel documents are subject to a $150 USD no-show/rebooking administrative penalty.

[SECTION 2: DOMESTIC TRAVEL & INTER-FLIGHT TRANSIT / LAYOVER POLICY]
For domestic flights, passengers are advised to arrive at the airport at least 2 hours before departure. Domestic check-in counters close 45 minutes prior to departure, and boarding gates close 15 minutes before departure. Acceptable identification for domestic travel includes a valid National ID Card, driving license, official government ID, digital ID via the official government mobile application (ThaiD), or a valid passport (birth certificates are accepted for children under 7 years old). Passengers connecting between two flights on a single through-ticket (Minimum Connection Time: 75 minutes for Domestic-to-International or International-to-Domestic; 60 minutes for International-to-International) will have their baggage checked through to the final destination automatically, provided they clear transfer immigration and CIQ (Customs, Immigration, and Quarantine) at the designated Transfer Desk in Concourse C. Passengers traveling on separate self-transfer tickets must exit airside, clear immigration, collect their bags at the baggage carousel, and re-check in at the departure hall. Transit passengers with layovers exceeding 8 hours may remain airside for up to 24 hours without a transit visa (subject to nationality restrictions) or book the Airside Transit Hotel in Concourse G.

[SECTION 3: CARRY-ON CABIN BAGGAGE, PERSONAL ITEMS & SPECIAL CABIN ITEMS]
Every passenger (excluding infants not occupying a seat) is permitted 1 standard carry-on cabin bag with maximum dimensions of 56 cm x 36 cm x 23 cm (22" x 14" x 9", including wheels and handles). The maximum cabin bag weight is 7 kg for Economy and Premium Economy Class, and 10 kg (up to 2 pieces) for Business and First Class. In addition to the main cabin bag, each passenger may bring 1 small personal item free of charge (maximum 40 cm x 30 cm x 15 cm, weight up to 3 kg), such as a laptop bag, handbag, small backpack, camera bag, or duty-free shopping bag, which must fit safely under the seat in front. Foldable baby strollers and infant car seats may be brought to the aircraft door for free gate-checking or stored in the overhead bin if their folded dimensions do not exceed standard cabin size. Small musical instruments (such as violins or ukuleles under 100 cm total linear dimensions) may replace the standard carry-on bag; larger instruments (such as cellos or guitars) require either purchasing an extra "Cabin Baggage Seat" (CBBG) or checking the item in a hard-shell flight case. Cabin bags exceeding size or weight limits at the boarding gate will be forcibly gate-checked into the cargo hold for a gate-handling fee of $65 USD per item.

[SECTION 4: CHECKED BAGGAGE ALLOWANCES, SPORTS EQUIPMENT & EXCESS FEES]
Checked baggage allowances depend on route and cabin class:
- Domestic Routes: Economy Class receives 20 kg (1 piece); Premium Economy receives 25 kg (2 pieces); Business Class receives 40 kg (2 pieces); First Class receives 50 kg (3 pieces).
- International Routes (Weight Concept): Economy Class receives 30 kg (up to 2 pieces combined); Premium Economy receives 35 kg (2 pieces); Business Class receives 40 kg (2 pieces); First Class receives 50 kg (3 pieces).
- International Routes to/from North & South America (Piece Concept): Economy Class is allowed 2 pieces up to 23 kg each; Business/First Class is allowed 2 pieces up to 32 kg each.
Under international occupational health and safety regulations, no single piece of checked luggage may exceed 32 kg (70 lbs) or total linear dimensions (Length + Width + Height) of 158 cm (62 inches). Bags heavier than 32 kg must be repacked into two bags or shipped as Air Cargo. Standard excess baggage is charged at $15 USD per kg on domestic routes and $35 USD per kg on international flights (or $150 USD per extra piece on Piece Concept routes). Pre-purchasing extra baggage online at least 24 hours before departure grants a 30% discount. Sports equipment (golf bags, bicycles in hard cases, diving gear, skis, and surfboards under 250 cm) counts toward the passenger's standard checked weight allowance; if total weight exceeds the free allowance, a flat Special Sports Equipment Handling Fee of $75 USD (domestic) or $120 USD (international) applies for up to 23 kg.

[SECTION 5: SECURITY SCREENING, LIQUIDS (LAGs), MEDICATIONS & DUTY-FREE BAGS]
All departing and transit passengers must pass mandatory X-ray and body scanner screening before entering the airside boarding zones. Liquids, Aerosols, and Gels (LAGs)—including beverages, soups, perfumes, lotions, toothpaste, mascara, jams, and Durian/fermented pastes—carried in cabin baggage must be in individual containers of no more than 100 ml (3.4 oz). All containers must fit comfortably inside a single transparent, resealable 1-liter plastic bag (maximum dimensions 20 cm x 20 cm), limited to 1 bag per passenger.
Exemptions to the 100 ml limit apply strictly to:
1. Essential liquid prescription medications, insulin, and cooling gel packs required during the flight, provided the passenger carries a matching medical prescription or physician's certificate in their name. Hypodermic needles and EpiPens are permitted in the cabin with supporting medical documentation.
2. Baby milk, infant formula, sterilized water, and expressed breast milk in reasonable quantities for the flight duration when traveling with an infant or child under 3 years old ( breast milk may be carried up to 2 liters even when traveling without the infant, though ice packs must be completely frozen solid at the security checkpoint).
3. Duty-free liquids (alcohol and cosmetics) purchased at an airport or onboard an aircraft, provided they remain sealed inside an ICAO-approved Security Tamper-Evident Bag (STEB) with the original itemized purchase receipt visible inside the bag and dated within the last 48 hours.

[SECTION 6: DANGEROUS GOODS, POWER BANKS, LITHIUM BATTERIES, VAPES & PROHIBITED ITEMS]
For flight safety, strict regulations govern batteries, electronic devices, and hazardous materials:
- Power Banks & Spare Lithium-Ion Batteries: Strictly PROHIBITED in checked baggage. They must be carried in carry-on cabin baggage only. Each battery must have a clearly printed capacity label (batteries with worn-off or unmarked capacity labels will be confiscated). Batteries rated up to 100 Wh (approx. 20,000 mAh at 5V or 27,000 mAh at 3.7V) are allowed up to 20 units per passenger without airline approval. Batteries between 101 Wh and 160 Wh (up to approx. 32,000 mAh) are limited to 2 units per passenger and require check-in airline approval. Batteries exceeding 160 Wh are completely banned from both cabin and checked baggage. Using or charging power banks during taxi, takeoff, and landing is prohibited.
- Smart Luggage: Bags equipped with integrated lithium batteries may only be checked in if the battery is physically removed and carried in the passenger cabin. Smart bags with non-removable lithium batteries exceeding 0.3g lithium metal or 2.7 Wh are forbidden on the aircraft.
- E-Cigarettes, Vapes & Lighters: Electronic cigarettes, vape pens, heated tobacco devices (IQOS), and refill pods are carried in cabin baggage ONLY (never in checked luggage), and recharging or using them onboard or inside the terminal is strictly illegal. Under Thai customs and consumer protection law, importing e-cigarettes/vapes into Thailand is illegal and subject to confiscation and fines upon arrival. Passengers may carry at most 1 small disposable pocket lighter or 1 book of safety matches on their person (prohibited in both carry-on and checked bags); windproof "blue flame" torch lighters and strike-anywhere matches are strictly prohibited.
- Sharp Objects, Weapons & Restricted Food: Scissors with blades longer than 6 cm, pocket knives, box cutters, multi-tools, martial arts equipment, and full-sized tripods exceeding 60 cm when folded must be packed in checked luggage. Firearms and ammunition require prior police clearance and Airline Security Declaration at least 72 hours before departure, packed unloaded in a locked hard case (maximum 5 kg gross ammunition weight per passenger). Strongly odorous foods (such as fresh Durian, Champada, and fermented fish/Pla Ra) are banned from both cabin and checked baggage on all aircraft.

[SECTION 7: LOST, DELAYED OR DAMAGED BAGGAGE CLAIMS & COMPENSATION]
If checked baggage does not arrive on the carousel or arrives visibly damaged, passengers must report immediately to the Baggage Services Office (Lost & Found Counter) in the Baggage Claim Hall before exiting the customs area to file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) and receive a 10-digit WorldTracer tracking reference number.
- Claim Deadlines: Physical baggage damage or missing contents must be reported in writing within 7 days of baggage receipt. Delayed baggage must be reported within 21 days from the date the bag was placed at the passenger's disposal. A bag that remains untraced after 21 consecutive days is officially declared permanently lost.
- Interim Emergency Expenses (First-Need Reimbursement): When checked baggage is delayed on an outbound trip away from the passenger's country or city of permanent residence, the airline provides an immediate overnight kit and reimburses essential toiletries and clothing purchases up to $75 USD per day for Economy Class and $150 USD per day for Business/First Class, for up to 3 days (maximum $225 USD / $450 USD respectively upon submission of itemized receipts).
- Permanent Loss Liability: Compensation for permanently lost or destroyed baggage is governed by the Montreal Convention and capped at 1,288 Special Drawing Rights (SDR)—approximately $1,700 USD per passenger—unless the passenger declared a higher value at check-in and paid the Valuation Surcharge (0.5% of declared value above $1,700 USD, up to a maximum valuation of $5,000 USD). Airlines are not liable for fragile items, cash, jewelry, laptops, passports, or negotiable bonds packed inside checked luggage.

[SECTION 8: FLIGHT DELAYS, CANCELLATIONS, OVERBOOKING & TICKET REFUNDS]
Passenger rights during flight disruptions caused by operational or carrier-controlled factors are as follows:
- Delays of 2 to 5 Hours: Free meal and beverage vouchers valued at 350 THB ($10 USD) per passenger, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi access codes, and free telephone/email communication assistance.
- Delays Exceeding 5 Hours or Overnight Cancellations: In addition to meals, passengers receive complimentary 4-star hotel accommodation with round-trip shuttle transfers, plus statutory compensation: 1,500 THB ($45 USD) cash or 2,500 THB travel credit for domestic flights; $120 USD cash (or $200 USD travel credit) for international flights up to 3,500 km; and $250 USD cash (or $400 USD travel credit) for international flights exceeding 3,500 km. Passengers may also opt to cancel their journey for a 100% full refund of the unused ticket portion (including non-refundable fares) or free rebooking onto the next available flight (including partner airlines).
- Involuntary Denied Boarding (Overbooking): If a flight is oversold, the airline must first solicit volunteers willing to surrender their seats in exchange for travel vouchers ($300–$600 USD), hotel lodging, and confirmed seats on the next flight. If insufficient volunteers come forward, involuntarily bumped passengers receive priority rebooking plus 200% of the one-way fare (capped at $400 USD for delays under 4 hours, or $800 USD for arrival delays over 4 hours on international routes).
- Force Majeure Exception: Statutory cash compensation and hotel obligations do not apply if the disruption is caused by Extraordinary Circumstances beyond airline control, such as typhoons, volcanic ash, severe weather closures, Air Traffic Control (ATC) strikes, medical diversions, or security alerts.
- Voluntary Ticket Refunds & 24-Hour Grace Period: Tickets booked directly via the official website at least 7 days before departure may be cancelled within 24 hours of purchase for a 100% full refund without penalty. Outside the 24-hour window, voluntary changes and refunds depend on fare family rules (Saver fares are non-refundable except for unused airport taxes; Flex fares allow free date changes and refunds subject to a $50 USD processing fee).

[SECTION 9: CUSTOMS, CURRENCY DECLARATION, VAT REFUND & DUTY-FREE LIMITS]
All arriving and departing international travelers must comply with National Customs and Excise laws:
- Currency & Gold Reporting: Any traveler entering or leaving the country carrying foreign bank notes, traveler's checks, or negotiable monetary instruments exceeding an aggregate value of $20,000 USD (or equivalent), OR Thai currency exceeding 450,000 THB (up to 2,000,000 THB for bordering countries), must file Form CD-104 with Customs officers at the Red Channel prior to departure or clearance. Importing or exporting gold bullion without an Ministry of Finance license is prohibited.
- Duty-Free Allowances (Green Channel): Arriving passengers aged 20 or older may bring in duty-free up to 1 liter of alcoholic beverages (wine, beer, or spirits combined) and 200 cigarettes (or 250 grams of cigars/loose tobacco), plus personal effects worth up to 20,000 THB. Exceeding these limits without declaring at the Red Channel results in confiscation and a penalty equal to 4 times the duty-inclusive value plus tax.
- Restricted Exports & Imports: Exporting Buddha images or religious artifacts larger than 13 cm requires an official Fine Arts Department permit obtained at least 5 business days before departure. Bringing fresh fruits, vegetables, raw meats, soil, or endangered wildlife products (ivory, coral, tortoiseshell) without an agricultural phytosanitary or CITES permit is strictly prohibited.
- Tourist VAT Refund (7% Value Added Tax): International tourists departing by air who purchased goods worth at least 2,000 THB per store per day at "VAT Refund for Tourists" participating retailers (Form P.P.10) must first present high-value luxury items (jewelry, watches, eyeglasses, handbags, belts, or smartphones priced at 40,000 THB or more per item, or total claims exceeding 100,000 THB) for physical inspection and customs stamp at the Customs VAT Refund Counter in the Departure Hall (Row W) BEFORE checking in their luggage. After passing Immigration, passengers collect their refund at the Airside VAT Refund Counter (Concourse D) in cash (up to 30,000 THB, less a 100 THB fee) or via credit card/Alipay/WeChat Pay transfer.

[SECTION 10: SPECIAL ASSISTANCE, PREGNANT PASSENGERS, ALLERGIES & UNACCOMPANIED MINORS]
- Expectant Mothers (Pregnancy Policy): Passengers with uncomplicated single pregnancies may travel without medical clearance up to the end of the 28th week of gestation (end of 26th week for twins/multiple pregnancies). Between 29 and 35 weeks (27 to 32 weeks for multiples), passengers must present an original "Fit-to-Fly" medical certificate signed by an obstetrician within 72 hours prior to departure specifying the estimated due date and confirming no risk of premature labor. Expectant mothers are not permitted to fly from the 36th week of pregnancy onward (33rd week for multiples) or within 7 days after childbirth.
- Unaccompanied Minors (UMNR): Children under 5 years old cannot travel alone and must be accompanied by a passenger aged 18 or older. Children aged 5 to 11 years traveling without an adult must be booked under the mandatory Unaccompanied Minor service at least 48 hours before departure (service fee: $50 USD domestic / $100 USD international per flight segment). Young passengers aged 12 to 16 may travel independently as young adults or opt into the UMNR escort service upon parental request. Dedicated airport staff escort UMNR passengers from check-in through security, lounge waiting area, boarding, and arrival customs until verified ID handover to the nominated guardian.
- Reduced Mobility, Wheelchairs & Medical Devices: Complimentary wheelchair assistance (ramp, aisle, and gate escort) and electric terminal buggies are provided when requested at least 24 hours before departure. Passengers may check their personal manual or foldable electric wheelchair free of charge in addition to their normal baggage allowance (battery-powered wheelchairs require battery terminal insulation and airline clearance 48 hours prior). Portable Oxygen Concentrators (POCs) and CPAP machines approved by the FAA are allowed in the cabin free of charge if battery-operated with at least 150% battery life of the total flight duration. Passengers with severe peanut or tree-nut anaphylaxis may request a "Nut-Free Buffer Zone" (3 rows ahead and behind) at least 48 hours before departure and are granted early pre-boarding to wipe down their seating area.

[SECTION 11: TRAVELING WITH PETS & SERVICE ANIMALS]
- Service Animals: Certified guide dogs and psychiatric/hearing assistance dogs accompanying passengers with disabilities are permitted inside the aircraft passenger cabin free of charge on all domestic and international routes, provided documentation from an accredited assistance dog organization and destination veterinary import permits are submitted 48 hours before travel. Emotional Support Animals (ESAs) do not qualify as service animals and are subject to standard pet transport regulations.
- Pets in Cabin (PETC): Small domestic dogs and cats (at least 10 weeks old) may travel in the Economy or Business Class cabin on participating routes if the combined weight of the pet and its soft-sided, leak-proof, ventilated carrier (maximum 45 cm x 35 cm x 20 cm) does not exceed 8 kg. A maximum of 2 cabin pets are permitted per aircraft, booked at least 48 hours in advance. The non-refundable PETC fee is $100 USD for domestic flights and $250 USD for international flights. Pets must remain completely inside the closed carrier under the seat throughout the entire flight.
- Pets in Checked Hold / Manifested Cargo (AVI): Pets weighing between 8 kg and 45 kg (including their IATA-compliant rigid plastic kennel with metal door) must travel in the pressurized, temperature-controlled, oxygenated cargo hold for $180 USD (domestic) or $400 USD (international). All international pet transport requires an Official Government Export/Import Health Certificate issued within 7 days of departure, a valid Rabies Vaccination Certificate administered at least 21 days prior to travel, and an ISO 11784/11785 15-digit microchip. Snub-nosed (brachycephalic) dog and cat breeds (including Pugs, French/English Bulldogs, Boxers, Shih Tzus, and Persian cats) are strictly prohibited from cargo hold transport year-round due to respiratory health risks, and may only travel in-cabin if they meet the 8 kg PETC weight limit.

[SECTION 12: VIP LOUNGES, FAST-TRACK IMMIGRATION & SLEEPING PODS]
- Airport Lounges: The Royal Silk & Sky View Lounges in Concourses C, D, E (International) and Concourse A (Domestic) operate 24 hours a day. Complimentary entry (up to 3 hours before scheduled departure) is granted to First Class and Business Class passengers, Star Alliance / oneworld Gold & Platinum tier members (plus 1 guest traveling on the same flight), and Priority Pass / DragonPass cardholders. Economy Class travelers may purchase walk-in lounge access for $55 USD (International, 3 hours) or $30 USD (Domestic, 2.5 hours), which includes hot buffet dining, alcoholic beverages, private shower suites (towels and toiletries provided), high-speed Wi-Fi, and business printing stations. Children under 2 enter free; children aged 2–11 receive 50% off walk-in rates.
- Fast-Track Priority Immigration & Security Lanes: Located at Departure Zones 2 and 5 and Arrival Priority East/West channels. Access is reserved exclusively for First and Business Class passengers, APEC Business Travel Card (ABTC) holders, diplomatic passport holders, flight crew, expectant mothers, passengers with disabilities or wheelchairs, infants under 2 years old (plus up to 2 accompanying parents), and senior passengers aged 70 and above. Commercial Fast-Track vouchers may also be purchased online up to 6 hours before flight arrival/departure for $40 USD per person.
- Sleep Pods & Transit Spa: Private soundproof Sleep Pods in Concourse F are available for rental at $18 USD per hour (minimum 2 hours) or $85 USD for an 8-hour overnight block.

[SECTION 13: AIRPORT FACILITIES, PARKING, TRANSPORT, LEFT LUGGAGE & LOST PROPERTY]
- Wi-Fi & Charging: Free high-speed public Wi-Fi (`@Airport-Free-WiFi`) is available throughout all terminal levels for up to 4 hours per session after one-time registration with a passport or mobile number. Universal AC/USB-C charging stations are located at every boarding gate seating cluster.
- Left Luggage / Baggage Storage: Short- and long-term baggage storage counters are located on Level 2 (Arrivals, Gate 4) and Level 4 (Departures, Row P), operating 24/7. Storage rates are 150 THB ($4.50 USD) per bag per 24-hour period for standard suitcases, and 250 THB ($7.50 USD) per day for oversized items/boxes. After 30 days, unclaimed items are transferred to the Central Warehouse at 300 THB per day.
- Ground Transportation & Parking:
  1. Airport Rail Link (ARL): Located on Basement Level B1, operating daily from 05:30 to 24:00 with trains every 10–15 minutes to downtown Phaya Thai (fares: 15 to 45 THB, journey time 26 minutes).
  2. Official Metered Taxis: Located on Level 1 (Gates 3, 4, 7, and 8). Passengers retrieve a queue ticket from the automated kiosk; fares are strictly by meter plus a mandatory 50 THB airport surcharge and expressway tolls.
  3. Ride-Hailing Apps (Grab / Bolt): Pick-up is restricted solely to the designated Ride-App Pick-Up Zone on Level 1 (Outdoor Lane 2, between Gates 4 and 5).
  4. Inter-Airport Free Shuttle Bus (BKK to Don Mueang DMK): Operates from Level 2, Gate 3 between 05:00 and 24:00 every 20–30 minutes. The shuttle is completely free of charge upon presentation of a confirmed onward flight boarding pass or e-ticket departing from the other airport within 24 hours.
  5. Parking: Short-term parking in Buildings 2 and 3 costs 25 THB per hour for the first 3 hours, capped at 250 THB per 24 hours. Long-term outdoor parking (Zone C, connected via free 24-hour shuttle bus) costs 140 THB per 24 hours.
- Smoking Policy: Under national public health legislation, all indoor terminal areas, concourses, lounges, restrooms, and airside gates are 100% smoke-free (violators face a mandatory 5,000 THB fine). Designated open-air smoking pavilions are located strictly outside the main terminal building on Level 4 (beyond Gate 1 and Gate 10) prior to passing security/immigration; once airside past immigration, there are no indoor smoking rooms.
- Lost Property (Personal Items Left at Airport vs. Onboard Aircraft): Personal items lost inside the terminal, security checkpoints, or lounges are held by the Airport Authority Lost & Found Center on Level 4 (open 07:00–23:00, tel. +66-2-132-1888) for up to 90 days; passports are handed over to the Tourist Police and respective embassies after 48 hours. Items left inside the aircraft cabin (seat pockets or overhead bins) are handled separately by the operating airline's Baggage Services / Lost Property office on Level 2.

[SECTION 14: VISA ON ARRIVAL (VoA), RE-ENTRY PERMITS & IMMIGRATION OVERSTAY FINES]
- Visa on Arrival (VoA): Eligible nationalities applying for a 15-day Visa on Arrival at Suvarnabhumi Airport (Level 2, Zone 2 prior to Passport Control) must present: (1) a passport valid for at least 30 days, (2) one recent 4 x 6 cm passport photo taken within 6 months, (3) a confirmed return flight ticket departing within 15 days, (4) proof of paid hotel/accommodation booking, and (5) proof of living funds of at least 10,000 THB per person or 20,000 THB per family in cash. The official VoA fee is 2,000 THB payable strictly in Thai Baht cash (an Express VoA Fast-Track lane is available for an additional 200 THB service fee).
- Re-Entry Permits: Foreign residents holding valid Single-Entry Thai Visas (Non-Immigrant B, O, ED, or Retirement extensions) who wish to travel abroad without voiding their existing visa stay may apply for a Re-Entry Permit (Form TM.8) at the 24-hour Airport Re-Entry Permit Desk located in Departure Zones 2 and 3 (after security, immediately before Immigration). The fee is 1,000 THB for a Single Re-Entry Permit and 3,800 THB for a Multiple Re-Entry Permit (one 4 x 6 cm photo required; on-site photo booth costs 200 THB).
- Immigration Overstay Penalties: Foreign travelers who remain in the country past their stamped visa expiration date must report to the Overstay Clearance Desk at Departure Immigration. The statutory fine is 500 THB per day of overstay, capped at a maximum fine of 20,000 THB (40+ days). Passengers with a voluntary minor overstay of 1 day (less than 24 hours) at the airport are generally granted a fee waiver with an administrative warning stamp. However, passengers voluntarily surrendering at the airport with an overstay exceeding 90 days face mandatory blacklisting bans from re-entering the country: 1-year ban for overstays > 90 days; 3-year ban for > 1 year; 5-year ban for > 3 years; and 10-year ban for > 5 years.

[SECTION 15: TICKET NAME CORRECTIONS, INFANTS, BASSINETS & EXIT ROW SEATING]
- Ticket Name & Spelling Corrections: Minor spelling corrections of up to 3 characters (e.g., transposed letters or phonetic typos) where the passenger identity remains unchanged are permitted up to 6 hours before departure for a $30 USD re-issuance fee (free within 24 hours of booking). Omitting a middle name does not prevent boarding as long as the First Name and Surname match the passport's Machine-Readable Zone (MRZ). Legal name changes due to marriage or divorce are processed free of charge upon uploading an official government certificate. Full ticket transfers to a different person are strictly prohibited on all fare types.
- Infants (INF) & Child Fares (CHD): Newborn babies must be at least 7 days old and born at full term without complications to be accepted for air travel. Infants aged 7 days to 23 months traveling on an adult's lap (1 infant per adult aged 18+) pay 10% of the adult base fare plus taxes and receive a free 10 kg checked baggage allowance plus 1 collapsible stroller. Wall-mounted aircraft baby bassinets (bulkhead seats) can be reserved free of charge up to 24 hours before departure for infants weighing no more than 11 kg (24 lbs) and measuring under 75 cm in length. If an infant turns 2 years old before the return flight, a separate Child Seat (CHD fare, 75%–100% of adult fare) must be purchased for the return leg.
- Emergency Exit Row Seating: By Civil Aviation Authority safety mandate, passengers seated in Emergency Exit Rows must be at least 15 years old, physically capable of lifting a 23 kg emergency hatch, able to understand crew instructions in English or Thai, and willing to assist in an evacuation. Passengers traveling with infants or pets, expectant mothers, unaccompanied minors, passengers using seatbelt extenders, and passengers requiring wheelchair or medical assistance are prohibited from occupying exit row seats.

[SECTION 16: IN-FLIGHT SPECIAL MEALS, PERSONAL FOOD & ONBOARD ALCOHOL POLICY]
- Special Dietary & Religious Meals: Passengers on international flights (and domestic Business Class) may request complimentary Special Meals at least 24 hours prior to departure (48 hours required for Kosher KSML meals). Available meal codes include: Vegetarian Vegan (VGML), Vegetarian Lacto-Ovo (VLML), Asian/Indian Vegetarian (AVML), Halal Muslim Meal (MOML), Kosher Meal (KSML), Gluten-Free (GFML), Diabetic Low-Sugar (DBML), Low-Sodium (LSML), Child Meal (CHML), and Baby Puree Jar Meal (BBML).
- Bringing Personal Food: Solid foods (sandwiches, pastries, dried fruits, chocolates, roasted nuts, and cooked rice/meat without liquid gravy) are permitted through security in cabin baggage. Cabin crew can provide hot water for baby formula but cannot reheat passenger-supplied food in aircraft ovens due to hygiene regulations.
- Onboard Alcohol & Unruly Passenger Policy: Complimentary beer, wine, and spirits are served in international Economy, Business, and First Class cabins to passengers aged 20 and above. However, passengers are strictly prohibited from opening or consuming personal alcoholic beverages (including sealed duty-free bottles purchased at the airport) onboard the aircraft. Under the Air Navigation Act, verbally or physically assaulting crew members, refusing seatbelt instructions, tampering with lavatory smoke detectors, or smoking onboard carries criminal penalties of up to 10 years imprisonment and a fine of up to 400,000 THB.

[SECTION 17: DRONES, CAMERAS, DRY ICE, MUSICAL GEAR & CREMATED REMAINS]
- Drones (UAVs) & Camera Equipment: Quadcopters and drones may be carried in either cabin or checked baggage, PROVIDED all removable Lithium-Polymer (LiPo) batteries are detached and carried exclusively in cabin baggage inside fire-retardant pouches (up to 100 Wh per battery). Operating a drone outdoors within a 9-kilometer (5.6-mile) no-fly radius around Suvarnabhumi Airport without CAAT authorization is a severe aviation crime punishable by up to 5 years imprisonment and/or a 100,000 THB fine.
- Dry Ice (Solid Carbon Dioxide): Permitted in cabin or checked baggage up to 2.5 kg (5.5 lbs) per passenger when used to refrigerate non-hazardous perishables, provided the package allows carbon dioxide gas venting and is declared at check-in for an IATA "Dry Ice" label.
- Wedding Attire & Fragile Items: Brides and grooms may hang 1 wedding dress or suit garment bag in the cabin coat closet on wide-body aircraft (first-come, first-served) in place of their standard carry-on. Television screens (over 32 inches), framed artwork, and ceramics must be checked in wooden or double-walled corrugated boxes at the Oversized Baggage Drop (Row E and Row S) after signing a Limited Release Fragile Waiver.
- Cremated Human Remains (Ashes): Permitted in either cabin or checked baggage when sealed inside a non-metallic (wooden, plastic, or biodegradable) inner container that can pass X-ray screening cleanly. Passengers must carry the original Death Certificate, Official Cremation Certificate, and Coffin/Urn Sealing Certificate at security and customs.

[SECTION 18: MEDICAL CLEARANCE (MEDA), SURGERY WAITING PERIODS & SCUBA DIVING]
- Post-Surgery & Hospitalization Waiting Periods: Passengers recovering from recent surgery or acute medical conditions must submit a completed Airline Medical Information Form (MEDA) at least 48 hours before departure and adhere to mandatory minimum pre-flight recovery intervals:
  1. Keyhole / Laparoscopic surgery or Colonoscopy: Minimum 24 hours after procedure (provided all gas bloating has resolved).
  2. Major Abdominal, Thoracic (Chest), or Cranial surgery: Minimum 10 days after surgery with surgeon's Fit-to-Fly letter.
  3. Eye / Retinal Surgery (with intraocular gas bubble injection): Strictly prohibited from flying for 2 to 6 weeks until an ophthalmologist confirms complete gas reabsorption (cabin pressure changes can cause permanent blindness).
  4. Bone Fractures & Plaster Casts: Flights are prohibited within 24 hours of cast application; for flights longer than 2 hours within 48 hours of casting, plaster casts must be longitudinally split ("bivalved") to prevent compartment syndrome from cabin altitude swelling.
  5. Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction) or Stroke: Minimum 14 days post-stabilization with cardiologist/neurologist MEDA approval.
- Scuba Diving Decompression Rule: To prevent Decompression Sickness ("the bends"), passengers must wait at least 12 hours after their last single no-decompression dive, and at least 24 hours after multiple dives per day or any dive requiring decompression stops, before ascending to cabin altitude.

[SECTION 19: AIRPORT MEDICAL CLINICS, YELLOW FEVER VACCINATION & PHARMACIES]
- 24-Hour Airport Medical Clinics: Operated by Samitivej Hospital on Level 3 (Landside, East Wing, Room 301, tel. +66-2-132-0393) and Airside Concourse D (near Gate D4). Doctors and nurses are on duty 24/7 to issue emergency Fit-to-Fly certificates (consultation fee: 1,200 THB), prescribe travel medications, treat acute illness, and coordinate ambulance transfers.
- Port Health & Yellow Fever Control: Passengers aged 9 months or older arriving from or having transited for more than 12 hours through any of the 42 designated Yellow Fever endemic countries in South America or Sub-Saharan Africa MUST present an International Certificate of Vaccination (Yellow Booklet) at the Health Control Counter (Level 2, Concourses C and E) BEFORE reaching Immigration. The vaccine must have been administered at least 10 days prior to arrival; unvaccinated travelers from endemic zones may receive an on-site vaccination (2,500 THB) and face up to 6 days of medical quarantine monitoring.
- Pharmacies & Defibrillators: Licensed Boots and Save Drug pharmacies operate from 05:00 to 24:00 on Level 4 (Departures) and Concourse D (Airside). Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) are mounted on terminal pillars every 150 meters.

[SECTION 20: CURRENCY EXCHANGE, ATMs, TOURIST SIM CARDS & eSIM REGISTRATION]
- Foreign Currency Exchange: Official commercial bank exchange booths operate 24/7 across all Arrival, Baggage Claim, and Departure halls. For institutional market rates with minimal spread (SuperRich, Value+, Happy Rich), passengers can visit the currency exchange zone on Basement Level B1 adjacent to the Airport Rail Link ticket gates (open 05:30–23:30). By anti-money laundering regulation, an original physical passport is required for all cash exchange transactions.
- ATM Withdrawals: International ATMs accepting Visa, Mastercard, Plus, Cirrus, and UnionPay are located at every terminal entrance. All local Thai banks charge a standard foreign card ATM withdrawal fee of 220 THB ($6.50 USD) per transaction, with a single withdrawal limit of 20,000 to 30,000 THB. Travelers are advised to select "Continue Without Conversion" (charge in THB) to avoid unfavorable Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) markups.
- Tourist SIM Cards & eSIMs: Official telecom service centers (AIS, TrueMove H, DTAC) are located in the Level 2 Arrival Hall between Gates 3 and 9, open 24 hours daily. Under National Broadcasting and Telecommunications Commission (NBTC) security laws, all physical SIM cards and eSIM profiles require mandatory biometric face scan and passport registration at activation.

[SECTION 21: PRAYER ROOMS, BABY NURSING SUITES & PAY-PER-USE SHOWERS]
- Multi-Faith & Islamic Prayer Rooms (Musalla): Dedicated quiet meditation rooms for Buddhist monks/laypeople and gender-segregated Islamic Prayer Rooms equipped with Qibla direction markers, prayer mats, Qurans, and adjacent Wudu (ablution) washing stations are available 24/7 free of charge at Level 3 (Landside, near Elevator B), Airside Concourse E (Level 3), and Airside Concourse G (Level 3).
- Baby Care & Nursing Rooms: Over 45 private Baby Care Suites are positioned next to main restrooms in every concourse, offering sanitizing wipes, padded diaper-changing stations, 85°C hot water dispensers for formula preparation, and lockable breastfeeding lounges.
- Public Shower Suites (No Lounge Pass Required): Transit passengers without lounge access may book private walk-in shower rooms at the Concourse G Transit Hotel (Level 4 Airside) or the Basement B1 Boxtel Wellness Zone for $12 USD (400 THB) per 30-minute session, inclusive of bath towels, organic soap/shampoo, and hairdryer.

[SECTION 22: DIPLOMATIC POUCHES, VIP MEET-AND-ASSIST & SECURITY PHOTOGRAPHY RULES]
- CIP / VIP Meet-and-Assist Service: Any passenger (including Economy Class) may book the official Airport Butler Meet-and-Assist service at least 24 hours prior to flight ($95 USD per person for arrival or departure). An airport representative greets the passenger at the aerobridge gate with a personalized name board, provides private electric buggy transport on long concourses, escorts them through Fast-Track Priority Immigration, and assists with baggage retrieval to the curbside limousine.
- Diplomatic Pouches & Couriers: Official diplomatic bags bearing visible external seals of a sovereign Ministry of Foreign Affairs are exempt from X-ray scanning and manual opening under Article 27 of the Vienna Convention, provided they are accompanied by an accredited Diplomatic Courier carrying an official Courier Letter specifying the number of packages. However, the courier personally must still pass through standard walk-through metal detector security screening.
- Unattended Baggage, Bomb Jokes & Photography Ban: Leaving luggage unattended in any public or airside hall triggers an Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) security cordon after 10 minutes. Making verbal jokes, false statements, or social media posts mentioning bombs, explosives, guns, or hijackings within airport premises or onboard an aircraft is a strict-liability felony under Section 22 of the Air Navigation Act, carrying up to 5 years imprisonment, a 200,000 THB fine, and immediate offloading without refund. Photographing, filming, or livestreaming at Immigration officer booths, Customs inspection tables, or Security X-ray checkpoints is strictly prohibited.

[SECTION 23: INTERCITY COACH BUSES (PATTAYA / HUA HIN), CAR RENTAL & LIMOUSINES]
- Direct Intercity Airport Buses: Located on Level 1, Gate 8 (Ticket Counter 8). Direct air-conditioned VIP coaches depart to:
  1. Pattaya / Jomtien Beach: Every 60 minutes from 07:00 to 21:00 (Fare: 143 THB per person, including 1 checked bag up to 20 kg; journey time 2 hours).
  2. Hua Hin: Every 2 hours from 07:30 to 19:30 (Fare: 325 THB per person; journey time 3.5 hours).
- Car Rental Counters: Licensed international car rental operators (Avis, Hertz, Budget, Sixt, Europcar, and Thai Rent A Car) operate 24/7 on Level 2 (Arrivals, between Gates 7 and 8). Renters must be at least 21 years old, present a valid home-country driver's license alongside an International Driving Permit (IDP, 1949 or 1968 Convention), a passport, and a physical credit card in the main driver's name for a 10,000–20,000 THB refundable security deposit (debit cards and cash deposits are not accepted).
- Official AOT Limousine Service: Fixed-fare luxury airport transfers can be booked 24/7 at the green AOT Limousine counters inside the Baggage Claim Hall and Arrival Hall Level 2. Rates to central Bangkok are all-inclusive (covering fuel and expressway tolls): 1,100 THB for a standard sedan (up to 3 passengers), 1,400 THB for an SUV/MPV (up to 4 passengers), and 2,200 THB for a 9-seat VIP Commuter Van.

[SECTION 24: FREQUENT FLYER TIER BENEFITS, MILEAGE UPGRADES & INTERLINE ALLIANCES]
- Elite Tier Baggage & Priority Privileges: Passengers holding Gold or Platinum status in Star Alliance or oneworld (or Royal Orchid Plus Gold/Platinum) traveling in any cabin class receive: (1) Priority Business Class check-in counter access, (2) Priority "Star/Priority" baggage tags ensuring their bags are among the first 20 unloaded on the carousel, (3) an extra free checked baggage allowance of +20 kg (Weight Concept) or +1 extra piece up to 23 kg (Piece Concept) on top of their ticketed allowance, and (4) Priority Boarding Group 1 or 2.
- Standby Mileage & Cash Upgrades at the Airport: Subject to cabin seat and meal availability, passengers holding eligible Economy (Y, B, M, H class) or Premium Economy tickets may request an instant one-cabin upgrade at the Check-In Supervisor Desk or Boarding Gate up to 45 minutes before departure using Frequent Flyer miles (15,000–45,000 miles depending on flight zone) or Fixed-Rate Airport Standby Upgrade fees ($180 USD short-haul / $550 USD long-haul).
- Retroactive Mileage Claims: If a frequent flyer number was omitted at check-in, passengers must retain their physical boarding pass stub and e-ticket receipt to submit a retroactive mileage claim online within 180 days of the flight date.

[SECTION 25: SEVERE WEATHER PROTOCOLS, LIGHTNING RED ALERTS & FLIGHT DIVERSIONS]
- Lightning Red Alert & Ramp Suspension: When tropical thunderstorms produce cloud-to-ground lightning within an 8-kilometer (5-mile) radius of the airport, Airport Operations activates a mandatory "Ramp Red Alert." During a Red Alert, all outdoor ground handling—including aircraft refueling, pushback tugs, passenger stair boarding, and baggage unloading from cargo holds—is immediately suspended until 15 minutes after the last lightning strike to protect ground crew from electrocution. Passengers waiting at baggage carousels will see "Weather Delay – Ramp Suspended" on display screens; standard baggage delivery guarantees (first bag within 20 minutes) are suspended during Red Alerts.
- Flight Diversions (U-Tapao / Don Mueang): If severe windshear, low visibility, or runway closure prevents landing at Suvarnabhumi (BKK), flights are typically diverted to U-Tapao International Airport (UTP, 140 km southeast) or Don Mueang Airport (DMK). For short weather holds under 2 hours, passengers remain onboard while the aircraft refuels and flies back to BKK once the weather clears. If a diversion exceeds 3 hours or crew duty-time limits expire, passengers disembark at the diversion airport and the airline provides free luxury coach transport directly back to Suvarnabhumi Terminal (2 hours from U-Tapao) or hotel accommodation.

[SECTION 26: TAX-FREE SHOPPING (KING POWER), DUTY-FREE PICK-UP COUNTERS & PRE-ORDERS]
- City & Online Duty-Free Pick-Up: Passengers who purchase tax-free merchandise in advance at Downtown Duty-Free complexes (such as King Power Rangnam) or online (up to 60 days and at least 24 hours before flight departure) do not carry "Blue Label" duty-free items out of the downtown store. Instead, items are delivered in sealed bonded pouches to the Airport Duty-Free Pick-Up Counters located Airside on Level 4 (East Pick-Up Counter near Concourse D, and West Pick-Up Counter near Concourse F) AFTER passing Departure Immigration and Security. Passengers must present their passport, boarding pass, and Blue Pick-Up Claim Slip at least 45 minutes before boarding to collect their purchases.
- Duty-Free Allowances on Connecting Flights: Passengers purchasing duty-free liquids (perfume or liquor over 100 ml) at BKK who have an onward connecting flight at another airport (e.g., Tokyo, Singapore, Doha, London, or European Union hubs) MUST inform the cashier at the time of purchase so the items and itemized receipt are heat-sealed inside an official International Security Tamper-Evident Bag (STEB). Opening the sealed STEB before clearing the final connecting security checkpoint will result in confiscation of the liquids at the transit airport.
